How to Play Sudoku

Fill the 9×9 grid so that every row, column, and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 through 9 with no repeats. Select a cell, then tap a number below (or use your keyboard) to fill it in. Use Notes mode to pencil in candidate numbers, and Hint if you get stuck. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert difficulty to match your skill level — a new, unique puzzle is generated every time.
